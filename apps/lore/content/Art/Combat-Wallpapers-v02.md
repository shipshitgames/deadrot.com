# Combat Wallpapers v02

This pass is superseded by [[Art/Combat-Wallpapers-v03|Combat Wallpapers v03]].
It improved the first noisy wallpaper batch, but still allowed close Scourge
surface detail to become too dominant.

## Ground Rule

Wallpaper art is not a sprite master and not a game runtime asset. It should
show the world in motion: humans and Scourge fighting, weapons firing, breach
light hitting armor, and enemy forms readable at a glance.

Good combat key art should:

- read at thumbnail size;
- use three to six large combat silhouettes, not a crowd;
- make the human faction and Scourge forms separable by value, pose, and accent
  color;
- preserve the shared Deadrot grade: soot, iron, bone, ember red, sick green,
  restrained cyan/verdigris equipment light;
- avoid dotted, stippled, halftone, pixelated, over-sharpened, and
  micro-scratch-heavy finishes.
