# Combat Wallpapers v03

This pass is superseded by [[Art/Combat-Wallpapers-v04|Combat Wallpapers v04]].
It improved composition, but still kept too much gritty/dotted surface texture
in the ground, armor weathering, smoke, and ruin fields.

## Why v03

The earlier wallpaper pass still let Scourge material become the subject. In
close-up, parasite tendrils, bone, chitin, and green organs collapse into a
busy brown-black texture mass. That is useful for creature design studies, but
bad for wallpaper.

Combat wallpapers should feel closer to official game key art:

- big foreground heroes;
- clean triangular or poster composition;
- mid-distance enemies, not macro Scourge material;
- readable weapons, helmets, shields, and boss cores;
- broad red skies, smoke, ruins, and rim light;
- no stipple, dotted texture, halftone, pixel noise, or tangled close-up gore.
