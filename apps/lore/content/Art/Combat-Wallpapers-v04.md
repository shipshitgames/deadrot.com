# Combat Wallpapers v04

This pass is a useful correction toward promotional wallpaper art, but the
current active direction is superseded by [[Art/Combat-Wallpapers-v05|Combat
Wallpapers v05]]. v04 removed too much realistic warzone grit while still
letting the background Scourge glow break into speckled point lights.

## Why v04

The previous passes fixed composition but kept a fatal surface problem:
high-frequency speckled lighting. In wallpaper-scale art, Scourge cores,
backlit tendrils, sparks, and red fog can collapse into dots when the generator
fakes glow with tiny point lights.

Wallpaper art should use:

- clean official promo/render/poster language;
- big readable squad silhouettes;
- realistic warzone grit on large readable forms;
- cracked ground, smoke, rubble, embers, and weathered armor when they stay
  broad and cinematic;
- distant Scourge silhouettes with one readable smooth core glow or blade
  shape;
- smooth bloom, not speckled glow, on background monsters.

Rejected for this lane:

- dotted texture, stipple, halftone, pixel grain, dirt speckles;
- speckled bloom, tiny point-light particles, firefly glow, fake star fields;
- tangled Scourge closeups, micro scratches, photobash grit;
- anything that turns the image into a surface-noise test.
