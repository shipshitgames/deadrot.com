# Starblight Reference Moodboard v01

> **Direction:** take the pilot commitment and build identity of [Nova Drift],
> the threat-channel readability of [Geometry Wars], and the loadout legibility
> of [Brotato]. Reskin all three through Deadrot's locked comic-ink house look.
> **We are not neon.**

This board is a production test, not a collage of borrowed screenshots. The
three panels are original Deadrot references generated from [[CANON]],
[[DESIGN]], [[Style-Bible]], and [[Starblight]]. The named games below are
touchstones for interaction and visual priorities only. Do not copy their
characters, assets, UI, palettes, or exact compositions.

![Starblight reference triptych](/assets/masters/art/starblight/moodboard-v01/starblight-reference-triptych-v01.png)

## 1. Momentum is the pilot fantasy

![Momentum and pilot fantasy](/assets/masters/art/starblight/moodboard-v01/momentum-pilot-fantasy-v01.png)

**Touchstone:** Nova Drift.

**Adopt**

- A ship commits to an arc: thrust, drift, correction, afterburner.
- The flight path should be readable from the wake before the player notices
  the hull's nose.
- A single hull owns the frame. The pilot is the fantasy, not a cursor moving a
  gun platform.
- Builds alter how the ship occupies space: turn radius, wake, mounts, escorts,
  shield footprint, and ramming posture.

**Reject / reskin**

- Reject neon-vector light as the surface language.
- Replace rainbow trails with disciplined hellfire heat: orange at the engine,
  blood-red at the cooling edge, then black.
- Replace clean abstract space with the wounded industrial silhouette of the
  [[The-Skyhook|Skyhook]] and orbital wreckage.

**Production test:** at gameplay zoom, a reviewer should be able to point to
the previous half-second of motion from the wake alone.

## 2. Density must remain readable

![Swarm and projectile readability](/assets/masters/art/starblight/moodboard-v01/swarm-bullet-readability-v01.png)

**Touchstone:** Geometry Wars.

**Adopt**

- Threats read by motion channel and silhouette before surface detail.
- Dense projectile fields still expose intentional lanes and dodgeable gaps.
- The player, enemy shots, salvage, and enemies remain separable at thumbnail
  scale.
- Every saturated mark communicates ownership or consequence.

**Reject / reskin**

- Reject interchangeable luminous geometry and color-as-decoration.
- Player fire is a hard hellfire spear. Scourge fire is a wet toxic organ/glob.
  Salvage is a bone-and-ember diamond. Never blur those grammars.
- Scourge craft are infected **voidship hosts**: black chitin over ruptured hull
  plates, tendrils through engine seams, and toxic breach hearts. They are not
  clean aliens, drones, or platonic solids.

**Production test:** desaturating the panel should preserve the three channels
through shape; restoring color should make ownership immediate.

## 3. A build must have a silhouette

![Build and hull identity](/assets/masters/art/starblight/moodboard-v01/build-hull-identity-v01.png)

**Touchstone:** Brotato.

**Adopt**

- A run's build reads immediately from its body and mounted weapon families.
- Hull choices create different openings instead of acting as cosmetic skins.
- Keep the build inventory glanceable: few strong families, visible mounting
  footprints, and clear role separation.
- Preserve the pleasure of becoming visibly over-equipped over a run.

**Reject / reskin**

- Reject cute/comedic proportions, item clutter, and character-shaped loadout
  piles.
- The hull remains a credible Pyre/Warden machine: scorched gunmetal, bone
  ceramic, blood markings, and hellfire engines.
- No player hull, shield, trail, or HUD state uses toxic green. That signal
  belongs to the [[Scourge]].

**Production test:** interceptor, gunship, shield frame, and escort/carrier must
remain distinguishable as flat black shapes at their real camera scale.

## One-page direction statement

Starblight is a top-down arcade **pilot** game with a survivors draft loop. Its
north star is not a surface style. Nova Drift contributes committed momentum
and hull-defined buildcraft; Geometry Wars contributes ruthless swarm and
projectile legibility; Brotato contributes instantly readable weapon-family
identity. Deadrot supplies the actual world and rendering language.

The result is a violent playable comic panel in orbit: bold black contours,
flat value blocks, restrained grime, blood on gunmetal, bone identification
marks, and hellfire propulsion. Toxic green is never a general space-game glow;
it is biological evidence that the Scourge has rewritten a host. Saturation is
functional. Shape carries the first read. Surface detail rewards the second.

The locked camera stays top-down / slight oblique for the arcade field. It must
show a ship's heading, drift arc, safe lane, projectile ownership, and build
silhouette at once. Tilting farther into a chase camera would sacrifice swarm
coverage and make the shared survivors pressure harder to parse.

**We are not neon.** If a frame could pass for clean vector abstraction,
cyberpunk space, or generic glowing geometry, it has missed Starblight.

## Do / don't strip

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Show a committed arc with a hot-to-cool hellfire wake. | Make the ship follow a cursor without inertia. |
| Separate player bolts, Scourge globs, and salvage by both shape and color. | Ask hue alone to carry projectile ownership. |
| Give each hull one unmistakable outline and mounting footprint. | Treat hulls as skins on the same triangle. |
| Show Scourge parasite anatomy consuming orbital wreckage. | Use clean aliens, geometric drones, or green player tech. |
| Use comic ink, flat shadows, hard values, and controlled grime. | Use neon, cyan/magenta glow, pixel grids, halftone, or speckle. |

## Downstream lock

Every later Starblight visual task must hit these four reads:

1. **Momentum:** the wake proves the ship's recent motion and intended vector.
2. **Hull identity:** the body and mounts reveal the build before the HUD does.
3. **Swarm readability:** threat roles remain distinct under peak density.
4. **Bullet-heaven legibility:** player fire, enemy fire, salvage, and safe space
   never collapse into one effect field.
