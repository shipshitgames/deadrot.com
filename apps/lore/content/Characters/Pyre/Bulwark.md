# Bulwark

**At a glance:** [[The-Pyre]] heavy Purger · walking furnace, not a Warden wall-builder · slowest but most durable Pyre avatar, heavy cannon or shotgun · appears in [[Scourge-Survivors]].

Heavy Pyre Purger built to stand in breach pressure longer than anyone should. The old
package read was too clean, too white, too close to ordinary sci-fi armor. Bulwark should
feel like a walking furnace dragged through a dark-manga siege panel: a human shape under
too much scorched metal, carrying enough heat to make the breach blink.

## Role
- Allegiance: [[The-Pyre]].
- Function: heavy armor and heavy weapons for [[Scourge-Survivors]].
- Fantasy: walking furnace, not a defensive Warden wall-builder.

## Gameplay Read
- Wide silhouette.
- Heavy cannon or shotgun.
- Slowest but most durable Pyre avatar.

## Visual Motifs
- Broad scorched plates, skull-thick shoulder mass, and reinforced boots.
- Furnace vents in chest, shoulders, backpack, and forearms.
- Blood-hot red heat scars under blackened armor.
- Bone ceramic panels cracked from repeated breach descents.
- Cautery Cleaver as sidearm, heavy enough to read as a tool for opening bodies
  and stuck breach doors.

## Sprite Brief
- Views: front, side, back.
- Make shoulders and weapon mass obvious.
- Avoid square Warden engineering language; this should still feel Pyre-aggressive.

Pyre heavy Purger, walking furnace siege armor, broad scorched gunmetal plates,
skull-thick shoulder mass, cracked bone ceramic panels, blood-hot heat seams,
heavy shotgun/cannon with cautery feed, Cautery Cleaver at hip, Berserk x DOOM x
Gears of War x God of War tone as brutal mass and weathered kill-gear, no clean
white armor, no blue sci-fi glow, no copied franchise costume, no neon.
