## Visual Thesis

Neutral Pactfall objective boss: a Scourge arena mass grown over broken arena
wreckage, built around one central toxic-green breach organ that both teams can
read from a top-down/MOBA camera.

## Silhouette Lock

- Primary read: broad objective boss, wider than a duelist and planted in the
  contested center.
- Proportion: heavy arena footprint, asymmetrical parasite bulk, wreckage fused
  into the body rather than held as props.
- Iconic shape signature: a central exposed breach core framed by bone and
  rusted barricade geometry.
- Forbidden shape drift: upright demon portrait, clean humanoid boss, blue
  sci-fi armor, or generic melee brute without an objective core.

## Material And Color Lock

- Body materials: raw blood-red tissue, black chitin, cracked bone, rusted arena
  metal.
- Accent materials: broken barricades, arena plates, exposed cables, bone
  protrusions.
- Emissive color: toxic green only in the central breach organ and minor node
  echoes.
- Forbidden colors: magenta, cyan, blue neon, clean white highlights.
