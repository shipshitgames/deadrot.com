## Direction

- Human character masters target **Berserk x DOOM x Gears of War x God of War**
  as mass, grime, practical heavy armor, mythic silhouettes, and brutal
  weapon-read.
- Pyre characters should feel like furnace-zealot breach operators: aggressive,
  heat-scarred, triangular, and willing to die close.
- Warden characters should feel like industrial defenders: square, braced,
  hazard-marked, and built to outlast.
- No clean white sci-fi, blue visor glow, superhero sleekness, paladin armor, or
  copied franchise costumes.
