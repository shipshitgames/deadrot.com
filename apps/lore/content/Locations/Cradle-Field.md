# Cradle Field

**At a glance:** the old world's great civilian launch field, and the evacuation that mostly didn't · where the lift went up into a falling sky on [[Zero-Day]] and most of it never cleared · the first ground the [[Scourge]] breaches rooted · dead, held by no one · the surface half of *the night we lost the sky* · seen only in [[Starblight]]'s Zero Day memory run.

A cradle is for lifting something clear of the ground. That was the whole idea of the place: every lift-cradle and boost-rail humanity owned, racked in one field, pointed at orbit, the plan for the day the sky finally came for us. When that day came, the plan ran exactly once.

While the Skywatch burned over [[The-Lantern|the Lantern]], Cradle Field ran its launches straight up into a ceiling of descending breach-matter — carrier-hulks, spore-fall, raw void coming down through the gap the dead ring left. A few boosters cleared it. Most burned back. The rest never lit at all: queues that never moved, families strapped into cradles that stayed cold, a launch order that kept being given to a field that could no longer answer it.

What the lift didn't take, the fall kept. The first groundside breaches rooted right there in the apron, in among the cold boosters and the boarding lines, because the densest, loudest, most crowded patch of the old world on its last night was the easiest place in creation for something that eats hosts to put down roots. Cradle Field is the graveyard of the evacuation — and the blueprint, in negative, for every Resistance holdout built since: scattered, quiet, never all in one place, never all on one field, *never another Cradle Field.*

## Role in the war
A dead site that argues. Every doctrine humanity holds now is a reaction to this apron: the [[The-Wardens|Wardens]]' walls are built where the lift should have been; the [[The-Hush|Hush]]'s dark discipline is the opposite of a floodlit launch field broadcasting *come here, everyone, all at once*; even the [[The-Pyre|Pyre]]'s burn-the-source creed reads as people who watched an evacuation fail and decided waiting to be lifted is just waiting to die. It holds no ground because there is no ground left to hold — only the lesson stamped into it.
