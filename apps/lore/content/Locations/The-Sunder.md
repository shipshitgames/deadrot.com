# The Sunder

**At a glance:** the holdout where [[Timeline|the Schism]]'s worst night nearly cost humanity its last free wall · the [[The-Pact|Pact]] was sworn in the ash here, over a sealed breach scar that still runs east to west through the concourse floor · the only holdout neither faction claims as its own — maintained jointly under the Pact · permanent arena for [[Pactfall]] sanctioned matches · appears in [[Pactfall]] / [[War-for-the-Lanes]].

The Sunder did not earn its name from the [[Scourge]]. It earned it from the night the [[The-Pyre|Pyre]] and the [[The-Wardens|Wardens]] chose each other as the problem.

What was once a transit hub at a contested lane crossing — old-world concrete and re-poured steel, solid enough to anchor a Warden chokepoint wall on its eastern mouth — became the site of the argument that nearly finished [[Timeline|the Long Fall]] without any help from the breach. A Pyre cell arrived first and said a [[Chorister]] had rooted in the east transit stack. The Warden garrison holding the gate said the Pyre had no ranging data, no extraction plan, and no clearance, and they held the key. The cell's Cinder had a name for that kind of patience. The Warden captain had a name for Cinders who moved without ranging data. The argument ran for an hour.

The breach opened in the second.

The [[Chorister]] was exactly where the Pyre cell said it was. The [[CANON|Choir]] had already threaded a relay through the transit columns ([[CANON]] §5–6), and the swarm came through the eastern wall neither side had ranged, in formation, [[CANON|Choir]]-pre-aimed at the gap the argument had kept open. Warden guns waited for orders. Pyre burners sprinted past the line before the gate moved. The breach was inside the holdout before either faction finished deciding who was to blame.

They sealed it together without agreeing to. A Pyre cautery team drove two burn charges into the [[Chorister]] while a Warden line held the eastern arch through the fire. When the node went feral and the Choir fell quiet around its scar, the sealed wound ran east to west through the main concourse, roughly where the two captains had been standing. They glassed the worst of it, sealed it with fused silicate, poured new concrete over the top. The seam is still visible through the floor. Not quite flat, slightly dark, east to west through the center. You fight over it now.

## The Pact sentence

The survivors at the Sunder did not forgive each other. They did not resolve which captain was right about the east transit stack. What they did — in the ash, with the breach still hot and the local horde going blind around the sealed node — was cut one sentence above the scar and leave it without a name attached:

> **When the wound opens, the argument ends.**

That is the [[The-Pact|Pact]]: one rule, no credit. The [[The-Pyre|Pyre]] hold the Sunder's west gate and burn wing; the [[The-Wardens|Wardens]] hold the east approach and the signals post. No faction insignia flies over the main concourse. The Pact sentence stays in bone-white stencil on scorched black concrete above the sealed scar, re-marked every season by whoever is on rotation that week, because the chalk wears off and someone always repaints it.

Neither side talks about the hour the argument ran while the [[CANON|Choir]] rooted in the east stack. Everyone knows the number.

## The arena

The Pact's price for the Sunder is that the feud has somewhere to go that is not the gate. The main concourse — breach scar in the floor, Pact sentence on the wall, sightlines the original transit hub never intended as a killing ground — is the permanent venue for [[Pactfall]] sanctioned matches. The rivalry burns off here, under lights, over the same floor where the breach came through.

There are no clean facilities at the Sunder. The sightlines are wrong for any audience that isn't fighting. The acoustics run scorched-concrete echo. It has been rebuilt around the scar three times since the Pact, each version harder-used than the last. The [[The-Pyre|Pyre]] think a clean arena is an insult to the doctrine. The [[The-Wardens|Wardens]] find themselves agreeing with that, which they find uncomfortable. Both factions send their best here. The sealed scar is underfoot the whole match.

Champions who walk the floor before a fight and look down at it have nothing to say that the floor doesn't say first.

## Role in the war

A **holdout** — the only one held jointly by both factions under the [[The-Pact|Pact]] rather than controlled by either. It is not a production center, not a civilian shelter, not a signals hub or a staging ground. It is the Pact's **proof of concept**: two factions that do not trust each other maintaining one piece of ground because the rule that came out of this ground is the only reason both of them are still alive. Destroying the Sunder would not break the Pact. The Pact lives in the field, not in a building. But the sealed scar and the sentence above it are the only physical evidence in the war that the Pact has ever worked — that two factions with a gate between them can seal a breach together instead of losing the holdout and then the argument — and neither faction will admit how much that matters.

The [[Scourge]] has tried the Sunder twice since the Pact sentence went up. Both times the gate was already open. ([[CANON]] §6 — the Choir's radius, and why two factions who share one floor of a holdout are harder to isolate than two factions arguing over who controls the gate key.)

## Appears In

- [[Pactfall]] — the permanent arena; every sanctioned match takes place here, over the sealed breach scar, under the Pact sentence. The Scourge surge that can force a mid-match truce is set over the same concourse floor where the original breach came through.
- [[War-for-the-Lanes]] — the Pact's political anchor; control of the Sunder is never a contested territory objective because both factions have agreed it must not be. It is one of the few points on the war map held as fixed by treaty rather than by force.
