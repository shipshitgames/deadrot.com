# Deadrot Combat Wallpapers v03

Promotional wallpaper/key-art pass generated on 2026-06-17 after rejecting the
v02 close Scourge texture direction.

These are high-resolution PNG masters for lore review and art-direction
reference. They are not runtime sprites and should not be shipped directly in a
game bundle without crop/export to WebP, provenance review, and manifest
registration.

## Files

- `pyre-cover-art-wallpaper-v03.png` - Pyre promotional cover-art stance with
  readable human hero mass and simplified Scourge threats.
- `pact-boss-standoff-wallpaper-v03.png` - Human squad facing a distant Scourge
  boss silhouette under red breach sky.

## Direction Lock

- Aim for finished game-promo wallpaper, not creature concept art.
- Keep humans as strong foreground silhouettes.
- Push Scourge detail to midground/background unless a single boss is the
  subject.
- Use one or two readable Scourge cues: blade arms, core glow, acid sac, crown,
  or maw. Do not fill the frame with tendrils.
- Favor broad matte-painted skies, smoke, and ruins over noisy parasite texture.
- Avoid dotted, stippled, halftone, pixelated, over-sharpened,
  micro-scratch-heavy, or tangled close-up finishes.
