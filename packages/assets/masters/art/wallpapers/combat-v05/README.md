# Deadrot Combat Wallpapers v05

Realistic gritty warzone wallpaper pass generated on 2026-06-17 after
separating desirable warzone grit from undesirable speckled Scourge lighting.

These are high-resolution PNG masters for lore review and art-direction
reference. They are not runtime sprites and should not be shipped directly in a
game bundle without crop/export to WebP, provenance review, and manifest
registration.

## Files

- `gritty-warzone-smooth-core-wallpaper-v05.png` - gritty battlefield with
  broad rubble and a smoother toxic Scourge core.
- `gritty-warzone-matte-boss-wallpaper-v05.png` - preferred anchor with a more
  matte distant boss silhouette and fewer glow speckles.

## Direction Lock

- Keep cracked ground, rubble, smoke, embers, weathered armor, scorched metal,
  and realistic warzone grime.
- Keep that grit broad and readable, not full-frame texture noise.
- Render distant Scourge as a matte silhouette in fog.
- Render the toxic core as one smooth bloom, not many small point lights.
- Avoid firefly particles, speckled bloom, glowing tendril dots, stipple,
  halftone, pixel grain, and fake starfield lighting.
